about

I'm Catelyn

I’m a product designer with 2+ years of experience designing business facing and customer facing products. Beyond product and UX design, I love to do front-end development!

The intersection of design and development is one of my greatest passions and directly informs my workflow, motivating me to create designs that are not only beautiful but also feasible.

Why do I design?

I design because I believe digital experiences should be accessible, intuitive, and empowering for everyone. Technology has the power to make everyday life easier, to help people stay connected with the people they care about, and to provide support when it is needed most.

My goal is to design tools that not only solve problems but also enhance how people interact with the world around them, making digital experiences more meaningful, inclusive, and impactful.
