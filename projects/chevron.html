Chevron Client Project

Overview

For a three-month duration, I am a design and research associate for two projects for Chevron.

I work in a cross-functional team with 1 project manager, 3 other designers, and 6 developers. Together we are currently creating a geo-spatial web scraper application and an emissions report dashboard in the Spring.

After the completion of the projects, both products will be adopted by Chevron’s internal team to use.

The Problem

The current web scraper Chevron uses has several issues with inaccurate results, unnecessary content, and poor usability.

Team members at Chevron are unsatisfied with their current practice and are tired of having to manually edit and fix the results from their web scrapes.

The poor experience is negatively affecting their business performance and their ability to grow their industry.

The Goal

Create a web scraper for Chevron aimed to streamline the process of understanding market dynamics by providing accurate and actionable insights.

Additionally, use this data-driven approach to support the client in making informed decisions about infrastructure investments and market expansion.
